Fruit fly body model -- attribution and changes
================================================

web/data/flymodel.bin is derived from the flybody fruit fly model:

  Source    https://github.com/TuragaLab/flybody  (flybody/fruitfly/assets:
            fruitfly.xml and the .obj meshes)

Changes made by FlyMatrix (Apache-2.0, section 4(b)), by
tools/flymodel/import_flybody.mjs:

  - Units converted from centimetres to millimetres.
  - Each mesh baked into its body's frame (geom position/orientation and the
    mesh scale applied to the vertices).
  - Meshes welded and simplified with meshoptimizer from 272,550 to about
    25,000 triangles, then quantised (16-bit positions, 8-bit normals).
  - Only the kinematic tree (bodies, hinge joints with axis, range and spring
    rest angle) and the visual meshes are kept. Collision geometry, actuators,
    sites, tendons, contact settings and all physical properties (masses,
    inertias, fluid and adhesion models) are dropped.

How FlyMatrix uses it: the kinematic walker (src/kin/KinematicFly) poses the
model's skeleton with FlyMatrix's own walking controller and draws its
meshes. The walking behaviour is FlyMatrix's, not flybody's; flybody's own
controllers (trained by reinforcement learning) are not used.
