Data attribution and licence -- FlyMatrix for the web
=====================================================

The files web/data/brain.bin, web/data/drive.bin and web/data/motor_map.tsv
(and the neuron positions and activity they produce in this viewer) are
derived from the FlyEM Male CNS connectome:

  Dataset   FlyEM Male CNS connectome, release v0.9 (male-cns:v0.9, 2025-10-03)
  Creators  FlyEM, HHMI Janelia Research Campus, in collaboration with the
            University of Cambridge, the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology
            and Google Research
  Source    https://male-cns.janelia.org/
  Licence   Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
            https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  Paper     Berg, S., Beckett, I. R., Costa, M., et al. (2026). Sexual dimorphism
            in the complete Drosophila male central nervous system connectome.
            Cell. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015
            Preprint: https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.09.680999

Changes made (as required by CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)):

  - Neuron positions are quantised to 16 bits across the dataset's bounding box.
  - The neuron activity shown is NOT data from the dataset. It is the output of
    FlyMatrix's own leaky integrate-and-fire simulation, run on the dataset's
    connectivity (synapse counts and predicted neurotransmitters) with the
    giant fibre (bodyId 10001, DNp01) stimulated for 15 ms.
  - drive.bin holds muscle activations computed by FlyMatrix from that
    simulation; motor_map.tsv maps motor neurons to leg joints using the
    dataset's cell-type names and soma neuromere/side annotations.
  - Only connections of at least 2 synapses were used.
